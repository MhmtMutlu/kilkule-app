## In short

- **We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or advertising ID.** When you first open the game, it
  creates a player account for you; the account does not know your name and is recognised only by a **random number**.
- With that account, **your game progress, wallet and battle results** are stored on our server in Türkiye. We do this
  to run the game for you, keep your progress safe and **stop cheating**.
- **Your player name and avatar are visible to other players** in the league and ranking tables. The game gives you a
  name automatically; if you want to type your own, **do not use your real name, surname, school or contact details.**
- **We do not track you,** we **do not sell** your data and we do not show personalised ads.
- **Rewarded videos are always optional.** Google shows these videos. In every country we first **ask for your
  permission** on the game's own screen; if you say no, nothing goes to Google and you can play the whole game just the
  same. In Europe, if you agree, Google's own form follows.
- You make purchases through **Apple;** Apple is the seller and Apple handles refunds. Apple's signed transaction record
  is first verified on our server in Türkiye, and what you bought is credited to your account there. Our server then
  sends this record, under a random code we create for you, to our service provider **RevenueCat** in the USA for
  reporting purchases and refunds. Your device never talks to RevenueCat (Section 3.9).
- We keep data only as long as needed. If the game **does not connect to our server at all for 12 months**, your account
  is deleted automatically (if you have made a purchase, this period is **36 months**; your unspent Crystals are deleted
  with the account).
- You can get a copy of your data with **Settings → Account → Download my data** and delete your account at any time with
  **Settings → Account → Delete account**.
- Questions or problems? Write to us in Turkish, English, French, German, Italian or Spanish: **destek@mm-apps.com**
  (Section 14).

## Our principle: as little data as possible

We designed Kilkule to work with **the least data a game really needs.** We do not collect data we don't need; we keep
what we collect for as short a time as possible and, wherever we can, we cut its link to a person.

**What we never collect**

- Your first name and surname (unless you type them as your player name), your email address (unless you write to us),
  your phone number, address, date of birth or age, your Turkish ID number (only in a formal KVKK request, because the
  rules require it; see Section 10). Through iOS's age range feature, the game can find out on your device only whether
  you are under 18; this answer is not sent to us (Section 11).
- A password: your account has none.
- Your precise or approximate location: we don't ask for it and we don't derive a location from your IP address.
- Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
- **Your advertising ID (IDFA):** we don't show iOS's "Allow Tracking" prompt; we don't track you across other apps and
  websites.
- The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting tool; we don't record which screen you stay on or for how long.
  (Reporting of purchase records from our server is described in Section 3.9. If you allow rewarded videos, the
  diagnostic, crash and performance information that Google's ad software collects itself is described in Section 4.2.)
- We do not process **special categories of personal data** such as health, religion or ethnic origin.

**How we reduce what we collect**

- **A random account number.** Your account doesn't know your name. This is what Settings calls an "anonymous account" in older versions of the app.
  To be honest with you, though: this number and the game data linked to it are still **personal data** in law, even
  without your name (pseudonymous data). That is why this policy exists.
- **Your account number does not go to other devices.** In the tables, other players' devices receive a random **public
  ID** that is separate from your account number. Your account number leaves our server only to your own device.
- **No network data in sign-in records.** Our session records do not keep your IP address or device/browser details;
  sign-in security records are kept without an IP address and deleted after **1 hour** (Section 3.11).
- **Short retention.** Every piece of data has a time limit, and data whose time is up is deleted automatically every day
  (Section 7).
- **No ad software without permission.** In no country is Google's ad software started until you tap "I agree" on the
  game's permission screen (Section 4).
- **Purchase records go from our server to RevenueCat in the USA under a random code; your device never talks to
  RevenueCat.** We first verify your purchases on our own server using the transaction record signed by Apple. Your
  account number, player name, IP address, device information and game data do not go to RevenueCat; test (sandbox)
  transactions are not sent, except for short periods during setup and troubleshooting tests (Section 3.9).
- **Cutting the link on deletion.** When you delete your account (or it is deleted for inactivity), the few records we
  must keep are stored with your account number removed. These records can no longer be linked to anyone, so they are
  **anonymous;** only records relating to purchases are not considered anonymous, because they may carry Apple's
  transaction number, and they are kept with the link to your account cut. The record of the deletion does not contain
  your account number.

## 4. Rewarded videos and ads (Google AdMob)

Kilkule has no ads that interrupt the game. The only ad is the rewarded video you can choose to watch at the end of a
level when you tap **"Double the reward"**, and it is **always optional.** Rewarded videos are shown by **Google AdMob.**

### 4.1 How do we ask for your permission?

In **every country**, rewarded-video permission is asked **first on the game's own permission screen.**

- This screen opens **the first time you tap "Double the reward", before anything is loaded.** The same screen also
  appears when you want to turn on the Settings → Rewarded video permission switch. The two buttons are the same size ("I
  don't agree" / "I agree") and no answer is preselected; closing the screen does not count as an answer. The text of the
  screen appears word for word at the end of the KVKK notice.
- **Until you agree,** Google's ad software is **not started in any country** and **no request goes to Google;** no video
  is preloaded when the game opens either.
- **If you don't agree,** the video offer no longer appears and you are not asked again; the rest of the game plays just
  the same.
- You can change your decision at any time with the **Settings → Rewarded video permission** switch. To turn it on you
  first see the permission screen and tap "I agree"; turning it off withdraws your permission at once. When you turn it
  off, no new ad is requested, an ad already loaded is not shown and the game does not start the ad software at all the
  next time it opens. Withdrawing does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.
- If the permission text changes, its version number goes up and players who agreed before are asked again.
- **If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland** (Google determines this from where the
  connection comes from), after you tap "I agree" Google's certified consent platform (UMP, IAB TCF) **also shows its own
  form**; to show this form, the app connects to Google. Google applies the choices you make in the form: depending on your
  choices, no ad may be shown at all, or Google may show what it calls "limited ads", which are not personalised and do
  not store information on your device for advertising. You can change these choices at any time in **Settings → Ad
  privacy options**. If you withdraw the game's own permission, the ad software is not started, whatever your choices in
  Google's form.
- **Legal basis:** your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 5(1); for the transfer abroad, see Section 6) and the permission you
  give (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and the national rules on storing or reading information on your device). The record of your
  consent is in Section 3.8. If limited ads are shown although you did not give permission in Google's form, Google states
  that it applies its own legal basis to that processing; on our side, the basis is that you gave the game's own
  permission and asked to watch the video yourself.

### 4.2 What happens when you agree?

- The ad software starts and **preloads** a video you may want to watch, so that it starts as soon as you tap. If you have
  reached the level where rewarded videos open, this preloading also happens when the game opens on later launches.
- While a video loads and plays, Google receives the following kinds of information **directly** from your device: IP
  address (from which Google may derive an approximate location; we do not collect location), device and operating
  system information (e.g. model, iOS version, language), app information (versions of the app and of the ad software),
  how you interact with the video and technical diagnostic information (including crash and performance information).
  According to Google's own descriptions, this may
  include device-level technical identifiers used to cap how often an ad is shown and to prevent fraud; these are not
  the advertising ID (IDFA).
- Google uses this information to show and measure the ad and to prevent ad fraud.
- **What we don't give Google:** your name, your player number (account number), your public ID and your advertising ID
  (IDFA). To verify the reward, we add only a random, single-use ticket number to the ad.
- **We do not show personalised ads.** With every ad request the game asks only for non-personalised ads, does not use
  the advertising ID and limits ads to content suitable for a general audience ("suitable for all ages").
- **Age signal.** If it turns out that you are under 18, or if your age range is not known (Section 11), the game adds to
  ad requests the signal indicating that the request may come from a user under the age of consent; in Europe, the same
  signal is also given to Google's consent form. According to Google's descriptions, requests with this signal are
  processed within the limits Google provides for users who are minors. If it turns out that you are under 13, if you
  choose not to share your age range, or if you have not yet answered where a law requires the age range, requests are
  also sent with the "child-directed treatment" signal, which Google processes within the limits it provides for
  children. The signals do not tell Google your date of birth or age; however, which signals are sent may give a hint
  about your age group (for example, under 13 or between 13 and 17).

Google processes advertising data **under its own responsibility** (as an independent controller). Details: Google
Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our
services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 8. Deletion of your account

### 8.1 Deleting your account yourself

**Settings → Account → Delete account.** When you confirm, your account on our server and all data linked to it
(progress, wallet, results, move records, your player name, avatar, public ID, league records, permission record, reports
you made and reports about you) are deleted **immediately and irreversibly;** the game also starts over on your device.
Only these remain: wallet movements and anti-cheat records with your account number removed (up to 12 months;
anonymous, except those relating to purchases), purchase transaction records with the link to your account cut and
purchase move records with your account number removed (24 months) and the deletion record, which does not contain your account number (24 months). Deleted data leaves the backups within 30 days at the latest.
The link between your account and the random customer code at RevenueCat is deleted from our server immediately, and we
ask RevenueCat to delete that customer record (Section 3.9).

Without an internet connection the deletion cannot be done and nothing changes; you can try again once you are online.
If you cannot delete, write to us (Section 10).

### 8.2 Accounts not used

If the game **does not connect to our server at all for 12 months**, your account is deleted automatically, in the same
way and with the same results as the deletion in Section 8.1. We do this so that we do not keep data nobody uses indefinitely.

- **What do we look at?** The account's creation, its last sign-in, the last refresh of its session and the day you last
  opened the app; if the most recent of these is older than 12 months (36 months for an account with purchases), the
  account is deleted. When you open the app with an internet connection, this information is updated automatically; if
  for 12 months (36 months for an account with purchases) you only opened the app offline, the account may still be
  deleted.
- **We cannot warn you beforehand:** Because we don't take any contact details from you, we cannot write to you before
  the deletion. That is why this rule is also written in Settings → Account.
- **What happens next?** When you open the app again, the game opens a new account for you. Your wallet, Workshop upgrades
  and table records on the server will have been deleted with the old account; the progress on your device (e.g. your
  stars) stays on your device.
- **Accounts with purchases: 36 months.** For an account with a **purchase or refund** in its wallet history, this period
  is **36 months.** The longer period is there to protect what you bought and to keep the records needed for a possible
  refund or consumer dispute. If the game does not connect to our server at all for 36 months, the account is deleted in
  the same way, together with purchased consumable items such as unspent Crystals. You can get non-consumable purchases
  (e.g. the tower in the Founder Pack) back in your new account with the same Apple Account through "Restore purchases";
  consumable items that came with them are not granted a second time (Terms of Use, Section 4.2). You can delete such an
  account at any time as in Section 8.1.

## 9. Automated decisions

Some decisions in the game are made automatically, without human involvement:

- **Result verification:** The server replays the battle's move record with the same game rules. If the result does not
  match the record, it is rejected and that battle's in-game rewards are not given.
- **Daily limits:** Daily limits on battles, videos, reports and data files, and a limit on sending the same battle
  move record again, are applied automatically. If the same move record is also sent from another account, this goes
  into an anti-cheat record (Section 3.4).
- **Moving purchases:** How many times and how often a purchase can be moved to another account with "Restore
  purchases" is limited automatically (for real purchases at most 3 times in all and at most once every 30 days; for
  test purchases by a daily limit). A move over the limit is not made, and the game tells you so.
- **Name removal:** If **3 different players**, whose accounts are older than one day and who have won at least 5 levels,
  report a name, the name is automatically replaced with a new name generated by the game. You can choose a new name
  straight away, but you cannot choose the removed name again. At the next connection, the game tells you once, on the
  Profile screen, that your name was changed automatically after reports (for a change made by our team it says so
  instead). Every automatic removal is recorded for review by a human.
- **Weekly League gift:** Whether you finished in the top 3 of a group of at least 10 players when the week ended is
  determined automatically from the server's own ranking.
- **Inactive account deletion:** The rule in Section 8.2 is applied automatically.

These decisions affect only **in-game rewards, in-game items and your player name;** they do not produce legal effects
concerning you or similarly significantly affect you. **We do not profile you** and we do not build advertising profiles.
Still, if you think an automated decision is wrong, write to us; a human will review it. If RevenueCat's verification
with Apple contradicts ours, only an anti-cheat record is opened; what you bought is not taken back because of it. Under KVKK Art. 11(1)(g) you have
the right to object to an outcome against you that arises from analysis made exclusively through automated systems.

## 12. How do we protect your data?

- All connections between the app and our server are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
- Only server code can write game data; each player can read only their own data.
- Your account has no password; we hold no password or email address of yours that could be stolen.
- Other players receive a separate, random public ID, not your account number.
- Sign-in records keep no IP address or device details.
- The app contains no software from RevenueCat or any other purchase infrastructure. The access key for RevenueCat is not
  in the app and is kept only in the server environment; purchase records go to RevenueCat only from our server.
- Backups are stored encrypted; access to the server is limited to the authorised person and requires a key.
- Retention periods are applied automatically on the server every day, so that data is not kept longer than needed.

No system is completely secure. If a security breach affects your data, we will notify the competent authority and, where
required, you, within the periods set by law.
