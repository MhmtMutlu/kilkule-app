# Kilkule · Support

A clay-and-toy tower defense from the Stone Age to space. More about the game: [Kilkule](/en/).

## Write to us

Questions, ideas, bug reports and personal data requests: **destek@mm-apps.com**

Please include your **player number**: Settings → Account → "Your player number" (the button next to it copies it). Other players never see this number; use it only when writing to us. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.

You can write in Turkish, English, French, German, Italian or Spanish. We confirm that we have received your message within 3 working days.

## Your account and data

You can do these inside the game, without writing to us:

- **Download my data** (Settings → Account): most of the data the server keeps about your account, plus the game save on your device, prepared in one file; what is not in the file is listed in Section 10 of the privacy policy.
- **Delete account** (Settings → Account): once you confirm, your account and progress are deleted from our server immediately and for good, and the game starts over on this device too.
- **Link with Apple** (Settings → Account; version 0.1.0 (16) and later, optional): if you link your account to your Apple Account, you continue with the same account on a new device or after reinstalling the game. Your name and email are not asked for ([Privacy Policy, Section 3.14](/en/privacy/)).
- **Rewarded video permission** (Settings): withdraw or give your permission for rewarded videos at any time.

The privacy policy has the details and retention periods.

## Purchases and refunds

- If something you bought did not reach your account, first try **"Restore purchases"** in the Shop. The Founder Pack and premium tracks (while their season lasts) come back this way. Crystals are credited to your account; they aren't restored if you delete your account or start with another one.
- If the problem persists, write to destek@mm-apps.com with your player number and the order number on the receipt Apple sent you.
- Purchases are made through Apple; Apple is the seller and **refunds are decided by Apple.** You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund from inside the app.
- We verify what you bought on our own server and credit it to your account there. Purchase and refund records go from our server to RevenueCat under a random code for reporting; the app never talks to RevenueCat ([Privacy Policy, Section 3.9](/en/privacy/)).
