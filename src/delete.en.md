# Kilkule · Delete your account

**Last updated:** 12 November 2026 · This page sums up Section 8 of the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/); where they differ, the policy applies.

## Delete it in the app

- Open Kilkule and tap **Settings** at the top right of the Age Map.
- Under **Account**, tap **Delete account** and confirm.
- **If you linked your account with Apple** (version 0.1.0 (16) and later): Apple asks you to confirm once more. If you
  close the confirmation window, the account is not deleted. If you confirm, our server deletes your account and asks
  Apple to revoke the game's Sign in with Apple authorisation; for this, the single-use code Apple gives is sent to Apple
  once and is not stored. If Apple returns an error, your account is still deleted but the revocation cannot be made;
  you can then remove the game yourself from the "Sign in with Apple" list in your Apple Account settings.

You need an internet connection; without one the deletion cannot be done and nothing changes. Once it's done, the game starts over on this device.

## What gets deleted

When you confirm, your account on our server and all data linked to it are deleted **immediately and irreversibly:** progress, wallet (including unspent Crystals), results, move records, your player name, avatar, public ID, league records, rewarded video permission record, Sign in with Apple identifier, reports you made and reports about you, the traces of your names and your moderation records. The progress and settings on your device are reset too.

## What stays for a while

- Wallet movements and anti-cheat records with your account number removed: up to 12 months (anonymous, except those relating to purchases).
- Purchase transaction records with the link to your account cut, and purchase move records with your account number removed: 24 months.
- If there is a review case about your name, only an anonymous action record of it (report counts, the automatic action and the decisions; the name, the link to your account and the list of reports are deleted): up to 12 months after the case is closed. An open case is closed as "account deleted".
- The deletion record, which does not contain your account number (for an account linked with Apple, with the result of the step revoking Sign in with Apple): 24 months.
- Deleted data leaves the encrypted backups within 30 days at the latest.
- The link between your account and the random customer code at RevenueCat is deleted from our server immediately, and we ask RevenueCat to delete that customer record.

## Your purchases

Apple is the seller and Apple decides refunds; deleting your account does not start a refund. Non-consumable purchases (for example the tower in the Founder Pack) come back on a new account with the same Apple account through **"Restore purchases"** in the Shop; deleted Crystals do not. More in the [Terms of Use](/en/terms/).

## If you can't reach the app

Removing the app does not delete the account on the server. If the game does not connect to our server for **12 months** (**36 months** for an account with a purchase or refund history or linked with Apple), your account is deleted the same way on its own; Sign in with Apple cannot be revoked in that case. Removing the game from the "Sign in with Apple" list in your Apple Account settings, on the other hand, does not delete your account.

If you want it deleted sooner, email **destek@mm-apps.com** with the subject "Delete my account" and include your **player number** (Settings → Account) if you have it. Because the account does not know your name, we may ask for a short step that shows the account is yours, so that we never delete someone else's by mistake. We confirm receipt within 3 working days and answer free of charge within 30 days at the latest.
